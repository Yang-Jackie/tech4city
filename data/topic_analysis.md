# 1. NUS — Common Themes

The NUS dataset is strongly centered on **academic pressure, CS culture, and student emotional experience**.

### A. Academic stress & workload (very dominant)

* Heavy CS/module workload and difficulty
* Exam pressure, grading curves, SU (module pass/fail) strategy
* Study strategies and performance anxiety
* Fear of competition and GPA impact

### B. Mental health & emotional strain

* Sadness, disappointment, burnout
* Academic + relationship + life dissatisfaction
* Anxiety about future career outcomes

### C. Campus culture & identity

* Criticism of toxic environments
* Debate on campus life vs studying
* NUS College (NUSC) dissatisfaction
* SU culture discussions

### D. Social life & relationships

* Dating, attraction, interpersonal conflict
* Hall life gossip, rumors, social dynamics
* Friendship formation and loneliness

### E. Informal / meme / chaotic communication

* Slang-heavy chat, memes, playful banter
* Anonymous confession-style storytelling

### F. Career uncertainty & future anxiety

* AI/job market concerns
* Startup vs GPA tradeoffs
* Field-specific insecurity (especially CS)

### G. Sensitive / edge-content clusters

* Moderation/privacy concerns in groups
* Political/world conflict commentary (e.g., war discussions)

---

# 2. NTU — Common Themes

NTU content is more **socially driven and interaction-focused**, with less emphasis on academic intensity compared to NUS.

### A. Social relationships & attraction (very dominant)

* Crushes, dating uncertainty, flirting
* Breakups, regret, missed romantic chances
* Hookups and social/sexual dynamics in halls

### B. Hall life & campus social environment

* Hall gossip, attractiveness, reputation discussions
* Nightlife around campus areas
* Student interaction culture

### C. Academic/admin topics (secondary)

* Module selection and faculty opinions
* Exchange programs and internships
* GPA vs experience debates

### D. Emotional / behavioral extremes

* Stress from internships or life decisions
* Aggressive or hostile complaint clusters (including spam-like content)

### E. Identity & comparison culture

* NTU vs NUS comparisons
* School pride and rivalry
* Student stereotypes

### F. Casual / meme communication

* Informal chat, slang, jokes, fandom references

---

# 3. Overlap Between NUS and NTU

Both universities share several broad social patterns:

### Shared themes:

* Dating/romantic relationships and attraction
* Anonymous confession-style gossip culture
* Student stress and anxiety (though intensity differs)
* Informal meme-based communication styles
* Concerns about future careers and internships
* Campus social life (halls, friends, events)

---

# 4. Key Differences

### NUS is more:

* Academically intense and CS-heavy
* Focused on workload, GPA pressure, and performance anxiety
* Career uncertainty and technical competitiveness-driven
* Structured around academic identity and stress narratives

### NTU is more:

* Socially and relationally centered (dating, attraction, hall culture)
* More casual interpersonal storytelling
* More explicit focus on social comparison and identity
* Includes more lifestyle/chat and emotional relational content

---

# 5. Overall Interpretation

* **NUS ConfessIt** reflects a *high-pressure academic ecosystem with strong cognitive and career anxiety signals*.
* **NTU ConfessIt** reflects a *socially expressive ecosystem with stronger emphasis on relationships, identity, and campus life interactions*.
